Sub-AC 3.1 — Remove com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch launchd service
=================================================================

Requirement
-----------
> Sub-AC 1: com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch launchd 서비스 unload 및
> plist 파일 제거 (~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch.plist)

This is the first cleanup sub-AC under AC 3 (legacy_cleanliness). With
AC 2 having migrated `paris-rental-watch` to the container-direct
web-fetch path, the host-side cron job that produced the staging JSONL
is no longer wired into anything — it can be removed without breaking
the skill.

Pre-state (captured before mutation)
------------------------------------
$ launchctl list | grep -i paris-pap
-       2       com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch

$ ls -la ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch.plist
-rw-r--r--@ 1 whahn  staff  1530 May  8 04:47
  /Users/whahn/Library/LaunchAgents/com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch.plist

The plist was a StartCalendarInterval job that ran six times per day
(KST 00:55 / 04:55 / 08:55 / 12:55 / 16:55 / 20:55) invoking
/Users/whahn/Desktop/nanoclaw/host-helpers/paris-pap-fetch/fetch.py
under /usr/bin/python3, writing logs to /tmp/paris-pap-fetch.{log,err}.
Reference copy of the manifest is preserved in this evidence file
above for archival purposes; the plist itself has been deleted.

Actions taken
-------------
1. `launchctl unload ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch.plist`
   — exit 0.
2. `rm ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch.plist`
   — exit 0.
