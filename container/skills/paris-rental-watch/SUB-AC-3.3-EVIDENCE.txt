Spec

> .paris-rental-pap-failure.json 참조를 모두 제거 (소스 코드, 설정 파일).

Scope interpretation
--------------------
Sub-AC 3.3 explicitly targets "소스 코드, 설정 파일" (source code, config
files). Two reference categories are NOT in scope and remain untouched:

  1. Seed contract — `.ouroboros/seeds/pap-cf-bypass-20260510.yaml`
     (the immutable goal definition that specifies the cleanup itself;
     deleting it would erase the spec under which we are operating).

  2. Historical evidence files —
     `container/skills/paris-rental-watch/SUB-AC-3.1-EVIDENCE.txt`
     `container/skills/paris-rental-watch/SUB-AC-3.2-EVIDENCE.txt`
     (audit trail of prior cleanup work; mentions the legacy paths to
     declare them removed — the artifacts ARE the evidence).

What was actually edited
------------------------
Single live source-side reference, in `container/skills/paris-rental-watch/SKILL.md`,
lines 50–53 — a "Legacy paths" deprecation note that paradoxically said
"SKILL.md 어디에서도 참조하지 않음" while itself constituting the reference.

  --- BEFORE (lines 50–53) ---
  > Legacy paths (`.paris-rental-pap-staging.jsonl`, `.paris-rental-pap-failure.json`,
  > 호스트 `host-helpers/paris-pap-fetch/`, `com.nanoclaw.paris-pap-fetch.plist`)
  > 는 PapRentalCFBypassMigration AC 2 에서 폐기 — SKILL.md 어디에서도 참조하지
  > 않음. 페치 실패 카운터·사유는 STATE JSON에 인라인.

  --- AFTER (lines 50–51) ---
  > 페치 실패 카운터(`pap_consec_failures`)·사유(`pap_last_failure_reason`)·
  > 마지막 알림(`pap_failure_last_alert`)는 모두 STATE JSON 인라인 — 별도 파일 없음.

The replacement preserves the engineering-intent half of the original note
(failure tracking lives inline on STATE) and drops the legacy-name listing
that was the last source-of-truth reference to the deprecated files.
