# Triage heuristics

Rules of judgment for distilling material into memory. Each rule earned its place through
a real mistake — when your principal corrects you and the correction generalizes, add it
here as a worked example (what was written, why it was wrong, the rule).

## Seed rules (from the first deployment)

- **A dispute or an estimate is an event, not decision rights.** A stakeholder pushing
  back on a claim or giving a timeline estimate gets recorded as a dated fact. Authority
  and ownership framings ("X has the last word on Y") enter memory only when explicitly
  granted — by your principal or by an agreement. Otherwise leave who-decides open.
- **Background ≠ current practice; group traits ≠ member traits.** Never infer someone's
  present religious, lifestyle, or political practice from their upbringing, family, or
  the communities they belong to. Record the documented background; leave current
  practice unstated unless directly evidenced or stated.
- **Relationship tags need relationship evidence.** Someone appearing in the corpus is
  not yet a relationship. Multi-touch, two-way interaction earns a person file.
- **Cold inbound never becomes memory.** Outreach your principal never engaged with is
  noise, however impressive the sender.
- **Single-source claims stay provisional.** A fact seen once, in one channel, gets a
  Provisional marker until confirmed by a second source or by your principal.
